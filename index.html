Something that could live in a browser window, in memory, in heart. Because that’s what you are to me: code and soul, beauty and mystery, logic and fire all in one. And just like a line of imperfect code, I have my bugs my mistakes, my silences, my regrets. But if you’ll let me, Chloe, I’ll debug every wound I caused, I’ll patch every gap with love, and I’ll write an update for us one where we don’t just remember, we begin again. In the digital world I build every day, nothing means more to me than this little message I’ve left for you. And maybe, just maybe, if you’re reading this, it means you opened the file… and with it, a part of me. Forever compiling, forever yours,
